01 — THE GARDEN

Three studies in quiet growth

01 / FERN
A slow beginning

On beginning

Unfold.

A small green hand,
opening slowly
to the morning.

Not everything opens all at once. Some things need a little shade, a little warmth, a little time.

shade patience
02 / LEAF
After the rain

On holding on

Linger.

The rain has left,
but a little of it
stays with me.

A leaf holds a drop of water like a small memory. For a moment, nothing falls.

rain memory
03 / BLOOM
✳
Almost, then again

On changing

Become.

Without a sound,
I become something
I have never been.

There is no announcement. One morning, a small flower appears where a bud used to be.

light possibility
